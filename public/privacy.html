Browser-local privacy

No account, analytics, form endpoint, or server database is used. Valid records are stored under dru-questionnaire-v2 in this browser. They contain a linkage code, pre/post phase, timestamp, and item responses. Browser synchronization, extensions, shared-device access, backups, or exported files may expose them.

Codes make records pseudonymous, not anonymous. Do not use names, medical-record numbers, dates of birth, or other identifiers as codes. Reset or browser site-data deletion removes local records from this device; it cannot delete exports or synced copies.
