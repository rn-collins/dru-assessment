Research-use limitation

This implementation cannot establish learning effectiveness, behavior change, causation, clinical safety, representativeness, or publication readiness. A future live evaluation requires a named protocol owner; ethics and legal review where applicable; validated or justified measures; participant information and withdrawal procedures; a data-management plan; minimum-necessary collection; access controls; retention and deletion schedules; incident handling; and a documented analysis plan.

For cannabis or medication questions, consult a qualified healthcare professional. Do not delay or change care based on this demonstration.
